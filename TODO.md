# Seven Notes: Fading — Delivery Outcomes

Status: all outcomes remain open until their listed behavior or evidence exists. Unreal-only outcomes are explicitly blocked by the current browser-game environment; the Three.js alternative must not be represented as Unreal completion.

## 1. Launchable third-person prototype and Hollow

- The project launches into the intended opening map and exposes a repeatable launch/test process.
- Hollow is a playable sixteen-year-old boy, with no native note and no initial explanation of why he is different. He is curious, observant, emotionally restrained and not a warrior/chosen-one caricature.
- Hollow walks, runs, changes direction smoothly, stops/idles, jumps if supported, interacts with nearby objects and orients toward significant interactables where appropriate. Third-person camera orbits/inspects the environment. Movement, collision and control return after narrative sequences must be reliable.
- If custom character art is not feasible, use a compatible temporary character and clearly identify its procedural/placeholder status and replacement point.
- The visible starter must be playable rather than a concept document, mood board, design proposal, screenshot-only artifact or instructions-only handoff.

## 2. Recognizable Sa and its three districts

- Build a compact representation implying a much larger basin city through enclosing cliffs, distant buildings/towers, the partial Ring Road, inaccessible bridges/neighbourhoods, skyline silhouettes, distant life, and a central instrument landmark.
- The Strung Districts contain a spawn courtyard/residential route, dense walkable buildings, balconies/overhead connections/awnings/hanging strings, an identifiable instrument maker or maintenance space, central-instrument view and a route toward the Ring Streets. It should feel inhabited and sonically rich.
- The Ring Streets read as a broad curved road/civic passage belonging to a larger circular city, connect visually to the central instrument, contain civic/maintenance structures, show the route toward the Quiet Quarter, include at least one resonance interaction and clear landmarks.
- The Quiet Quarter uses wider, sparse, deliberate and acoustically distinct spaces, a small communal square, seating/platforms arranged for the Evening Minute, views of cliffs and the instrument, reduced activity and subtle Fading evidence.
- The monumental central instrument is recognizable as a tambura/tanpura-like instrument, not a generic tower, antenna, reactor or crystal; it has a monumental body, elongated structure, four spatially legible strings extending toward districts, supports/tension mechanisms/material separation, and is visible from at least two important viewpoints.
- Architecture itself embodies generations of resonance through coherent instrument-like forms, carved timber/stone/plaster, handcrafted joinery, layered courtyards/passages, bridges/cables, acoustically shaped openings and repaired/strained materials. Avoid generic European castles, Gothic clichés, random temple assets, cyberpunk, sci-fi, generic glowing rune decoration, plastic/neon/gloss and arbitrary musical symbols.

## 3. Sound, borrowed note, noise, Rest and complete puzzle

- Interactions are contextual and object-specific: a subtle nearby prompt appears; Hollow makes a short gesture/touches a resonant surface; a distinct tone plays; the target makes a specific physical/environmental response; nearby materials may respond sympathetically; effects conclude cleanly.
- Hollow can temporarily borrow one or more distinct notes from environmental sources, with clear audio/visual identity and at least one compatible physical use. Notes are extendable gameplay signatures, not ordinary ammunition. Prefer one polished note (and optionally a second), not seven broken skill trees.
- At least one reusable environmental object accumulates visible/audio noise under repeated or prolonged forced resonance, becomes irregular/unstable at an understandable threshold, and can recover rather than become permanently broken.
- Rest stops active resonance, temporarily reduces environmental audio activity, clears nearby accumulated test-object noise, changes local atmosphere and makes intentional quiet legible without a blast, projectile, offensive spell or sound wave.
- Include at least one complete, physically constructed, observable, reliable resonance puzzle that is understandable without a lengthy tutorial, solvable by observation and responsive to compatible sound; forcing sound must have consequences and Rest/resolution must allow completion. Avoid arbitrary colour matching, generic rune sequences, keycards and disconnected logic puzzles.

## 4. Opening, Evening Minute, Fading and playable hook

- Start immediately in ordinary Strung District life on Hollow's sixteenth birthday, with limited exposition, readable path and ambient workers/repairs/strings/central sound.
- Teach one environmental resonance interaction through the world, not a long tutorial panel. Guide exploration to the central instrument and Quiet Quarter.
- Residents/families gather and sit in the Evening Minute without an exposition dump explaining its history. Preserve its ambiguous surviving relationship to the lost Rest.
- On arrival in Sa, stage the Fading gradually: distant ambience loses richness; familiar sounds stop; hanging strings vibrate without their usual tones; color/contrast diminish; people seem confused/numb/distant; the central instrument produces a strained/incomplete phrase; the sequence ends with unsettling reduction rather than explosion, monster, battle, smoke or saturated corruption.
- Return full control to the player after the sequence, with an immediate objective and clear ending/hook. Do not reveal the complete ancient history, Hollow's mother/identity or the full role of the off-screen gentle twin sister. Do not turn the twin into a villain.

## 5. Restraint, UI, audio usability and meaningful world details

- Present main menu actions Start Game, Settings and Quit; a pause menu; audio controls; contextual interaction prompt; compact borrowed-note indicator; small objective; and basic movement/interact prompts. Interface stays restrained, readable and world-appropriate, with no oversized quest markers, sci-fi panels, inventory, ability wheel, damage numbers or excessive HUD.
- Provide adjustable audio volume and muting/reduction. The Strung Districts, Ring Streets, Quiet Quarter, central instrument, interactables and Fading have differentiated sound behavior. Interactions, borrowed note, forced resonance/noise, Rest and Fading dynamically change sound smoothly; essential puzzle information also has visual cues.
- Any synthesized tones/ambience are identified as placeholders, not claimed as finished original recordings or soundtrack. Use silence intentionally; no generic epic orchestral layer.
- Use a small set of purposeful NPC silhouettes/actions and meaningful environmental evidence (instrument repair, string adjustment, Evening Minute preparation, strained abandoned object, brittle support or newly absent street sound), not crowds of non-interactive filler.
- Cloth, strings, small objects, bridge/architecture and distant figures move purposefully; Fading can make familiar motion unreliable. Avoid constant motion and excessive particles.

## 6. Technical organization, performance and honest engine limitations

- Reuse modular/instanced construction where helpful; keep collision aligned with visible route geometry, interaction distances reliable, shadow/light/texture cost controlled and runtime stable on the available target.
- Maintain the starter's working input, fixed-step loop, physics, save/settings, renderer, Tweak parameter manager/preview bridge and build setup. Do not add unneeded plugins or systems.
- If the environment cannot run Unreal Engine or package the game, continue only with genuinely creatable project components and explicitly document which work requires a local Unreal installation. Never claim unverified `.uproject`, Blueprints, C++ or Windows package completion.
- Use only supplied, existing or authorized assets; inspect references if available; do not assume references. Do not claim generated concept art is a finished 3D model. Preserve provenance/licensing if third-party assets are added.
- Document exact project location, actual playable content, actual controls, tests and results, known unfinished/placeholders and the five most valuable next development priorities.

## 7. Verification and QA evidence

- Test launch, movement/rotation/navigation, terrain collision, accessible interactables, prompt distance, consistent musical feedback, puzzle completion, repeated-use recovery, noise accumulation, Rest, Fading trigger, control return and reachable ending.
- Review spatial hierarchy, readability of the instrument and all four strings from important locations, difference between three districts, material cohesion, Fading as sensory loss rather than generic corruption, identifiable interactables and gameplay-distance legibility.
- Review major audio interactions, Quiet Quarter distinction, Evening Minute and Fading changes, absence of stuck/unbounded audio and visual equivalents of major sound cues.
- Check missing assets/broken references, obvious collision issues, available code errors and repeatable launch procedure. Package only if actual packaging is supported. Record all tests that cannot be performed; do not fabricate results.

## 8. Original creative standard and scope discipline

- Every major decision reinforces: “The world did not begin to die because it lost its music. It began to die because it forgot how to let music end.” The central instrument is Sa's sustaining heartbeat; four strings connect it to districts; Strung is full of life and accumulated sound; Ring Street conveys civic order; the Quiet Quarter contrasts spatially and acoustically; the Evening Minute is a fragile memory; the Fading reveals sound unable to resolve; Hollow's unusual relation to notes stays meaningful but unexplained.
- The result remains dark-fantasy exploration, environmental storytelling and light puzzle-solving—not generic action fantasy, combat, second magic system, arbitrary lore, excessive exposition or unrelated effects.
- Do not build the full six-kilometre city, all seven skill trees, full open-world streaming, large dialogue tree, combat, multiplayer, inventory, complex enemy AI, full cinematic, every tribe/district or finished soundtrack before the core playable route works.

## Prototype progress and remaining work

The eight outcomes above remain open where any acceptance clause has not yet been met. Evidence below reflects this browser prototype only.

1. **Launchable third-person prototype — substantially implemented; still partial.** Browser launch, movement through the shared input adapter, interaction, restarting, jump/input unit coverage, and the human-style temporary Hollow have been verified. Final character art is still a procedural placeholder; mouse-orbit and all movement situations were not part of the production smoke run.
2. **Recognizable Sa — implemented as a compact procedural slice; still partial.** Strung, Ring Street and Quiet Quarter, cliff basin, distant structures, central instrument silhouette and strings appear in the preview/screenshots. The complete city, polished architecture, full 500–800m traversal, and formal legibility check for every string from two separate locations remain incomplete.
3. **Sound and resonance — implemented for the prototype.** The production smoke exercised borrowed Sa, two forced latch strikes, strain, a further unstable interaction, Rest recovery, and puzzle completion. Synthesized audio and responsive materials are placeholders; detailed audio listening and recording quality were not verified.
4. **Opening and Fading — playable opening slice; still partial against the duration target.** The smoke completed the Evening Minute, Fading passage, control return and trace objective without revealing the hidden family history. More exploration/story beats are needed to establish the brief’s intended 5–10-minute opening.
5. **UI, audio controls and world details — prototype interface implemented.** Start/settings/pause/ending flows, adjustable settings, compact HUD and touch layout were exercised or previewed. Synthesized tones/ambience are explicitly placeholders; final production audio, full interaction affordance review and motion polish remain.
6. **Technical organization and honest limits — browser implementation verified; Unreal is blocked.** TypeScript/build passed; modular Three.js/Rapier source and preview bridge remain. This is not an Unreal project or Windows package. Character/world placeholder replacement and a native UE5 port require local Unreal work.
7. **Verification — smoke and automated checks passed; not full acceptance audit.** `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm build` and `pnpm smoke` passed. The smoke covers movement via the shared input adapter, settings, borrowing, latch strain/Rest, Evening Minute, ending and restart. It does not verify Unreal packaging, publication, all keyboard/mouse/gamepad hardware, exhaustive collision boundaries, recorded audio or every city vista. The smoke produced files under `shots/`.
8. **Creative standard — direction is represented, not production-finished.** The prototype keeps the musical-restraint concept, limited scope, unresolved history, no combat or second magic system, and restrained dusk-fantasy UI. Final artistic quality, a longer authored route, professional animation/audio and complete story content remain future work.
