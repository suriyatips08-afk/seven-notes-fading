THE QUIET QUARTER

They feared what could not be held.

The People of Silence understood the Rest: every note needs room to end. They had no greater power than anyone else. They simply knew that a world kept ringing without pause would begin to break.

Their warning sounded like a threat to those who had mistaken control for safety.
